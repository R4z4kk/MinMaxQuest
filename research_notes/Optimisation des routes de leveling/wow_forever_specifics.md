# WoW Forever specifics for leveling routes and quest data (as of 2026-11-27)

Legend: **[PRE]** = pre-launch announcement or beta info (before 2026-11-04). **[LIVE]** = confirmed after launch (official hotfix post, live client data or live addon code). **[LOCAL]** = read from addons installed under `D:\Jeux\Battle.net\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` (read only).

Correction to the brief: the new race's English name is **Skyborne**, not "Celestial". "Celestial" comes from translations: "Célestes" (FR), "célicos"/"cielonato" (ES), "celonatos" (PT).

## 1. Leveling changes in Forever that affect questing efficiency

### Takeaway
Total XP to reach 60 is reported as unchanged and the cap stays at 60. What changed is where XP comes from. Dungeon kill XP was cut while dungeon quest XP was raised a lot, and grey (low-level) quests appear to keep 50% of their XP instead of Classic's 10%. There are also over 1,000 new quests, 4 new zones, 4 expanded zones with new map coordinates, riding at 40, and quest gold added at 48+. A route optimizer cannot reuse Classic Era XP tables or coordinates as they are.

### Cited Findings
**Cap and XP curve**
- [PRE] Level cap is 60 "indefinitely". The "Journey before destination" pillar says there will be no level cap increases, and players catch up only by leveling — [Warcraft Wiki: WoW Forever](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever)
- [PRE] Wowhead's 2026-09-21 article claimed reduced XP requirements, then retracted it. The new headline is "[Retracted] Experience Values in WoW Forever Same as Classic". The fetched page did not show the Blizzard quote itself — [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/experience-requirements-significantly-reduced-in-wow-forever-383038)
- Fan guides agree the curve matches Classic (about 4.1M XP for 1-60). This is an aggregator claim — [WebSearch summary of fan guides, e.g. MMONerds](https://mmonerds.com/wow-forever-leveling-changes-questing-dungeon-xp-1-60/)

**Quest XP formula** [LIVE][LOCAL]
- Questie's Forever branch clamps the level-difference multiplier to a minimum of 5/10 instead of Classic's 1/10. The code comment reads "On Forever the fractions are 100% 80% 60% 50%". So a grey quest still pays 50% XP on Forever, against 10% in Classic. The rounding steps (5/10/25/50) are the same as Classic — `Questie\Database\QuestXP\QuestieXP.lua`, `getAdjustedXP()`
- Questie v12.15.0 (2026-11-25) changelog: "[Forever] Adjust quest XP fraction modifier". The formula above was being tuned only 2 days before these notes — [Questie releases (GitHub API)](https://api.github.com/repos/Questie/Questie/releases?per_page=15)
- QuestieDB ships a separate Forever quest XP table, `QuestieDB\support\Forever\QuestXP\xpDB-traces.lua` (format `QuestXP.db[questId] = {questLevel, baseXP}`). Its header says: "Generated by QuestieTrace extract … Source trace files: 475220, Sessions aggregated: 2512170, Generated at: 2026-11-24T10:38:01.776Z". A Classic baseline `xpDB-classic.lua` sits next to it [LOCAL]
- Dungeon quest XP is much higher than in Classic [PRE, beta figures]. Examples: Deviate Hides 1,600 in Classic vs 3,100 in Forever; the Deadmines quests 8,300 vs 18,450; the City of Dalaran quests 15,800 per faction. On 2026-10-01 the extra dungeon quest XP was halved, though it still pays more than double Classic — [Method.gg, 2026-11-14](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-dungeon-xp-explained-and-all-dungeons-by-level)
- [LIVE] Nov 10 hotfix: "Most quests level 48 and higher now additionally reward gold" — [Blizzard hotfixes (cumulative)](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)

**Kill XP and group XP**
- [PRE] Dungeon kill XP was about 55% below Classic in beta, then raised about 20% on 2026-10-08. Party XP is now based on each player's own level, not the highest-level member's — [Method.gg](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-dungeon-xp-explained-and-all-dungeons-by-level)
- [LIVE] Nov 9: dungeon kill experience "increased by 50%". Nov 13: dungeon kills award zero XP to party members when the enemy is trivial to their level. Nov 10: Shatterspear Trolls no longer grant XP. Nov 16: "Water Pouch Bounty" no longer grants XP — [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)
- Conflicting detail: LFCarry dates the +50% to Nov 10, says dungeon kill XP is "60% of Classic Era", and gives a "12 levels above" threshold for the zero-XP rule. Blizzard's post says Nov 9 and gives no numeric threshold — [LFCarry](https://lfcarry.com/guides/wow-forever-patch-notes) vs [Blizzard](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)
- [LIVE] Nov 20: dungeon entries are capped at 5 per hour (was 10) — [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)
- [LIVE] Nov 13: quest items now drop for every eligible party member, and several party-credit fixes followed — [LFCarry](https://lfcarry.com/guides/wow-forever-patch-notes); [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)

**New race and starting zone**

The race is neutral by origin and the player picks a side at creation. It requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack ($29.99) or a higher edition — [Blizzard: Meet the Skyborne](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302071); [WebSearch summary](https://wow.gg/fr/guides/wow-forever-skyborne)

- Non-Skyborne characters can also play through Zephras Isle — [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-players-can-experience-zephras-isle-without-playing-skyborne/)
- [LIVE] Nov 19: Skyborne get a 10% riding discount at Honored from Genn Fairweather on Zephras Isle — [LFCarry](https://lfcarry.com/guides/wow-forever-patch-notes); a Skyborne riding discount fix is also in [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)

**New and reworked zones and quests**

- [LIVE] Questie release notes list removed Classic content on Forever: "Hide removed 'Hearts of the Lost' quests" (v12.10.0, Nov 17); "Hide classic 'Khan Jehn' and 'Khan Hratha'" (v12.11.0, Nov 18); "Blacklist 'Rekindle'" (v12.9.0, Nov 14) — [Questie releases](https://api.github.com/repos/Questie/Questie/releases?per_page=15)
- [LIVE] Nov 17: The Battle for Alterac Valley quests now require level 58, and Agamaggan's Blood is no longer a dungeon quest. Nov 9: "Find Bingles" moved to Loch Modan. Nov 6: "Unending Torment" is now level 15 (was 16) — [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281); [LFCarry](https://lfcarry.com/guides/wow-forever-patch-notes)

**Mounts, travel, flight paths**
- [PRE] Apprentice riding: level 40, 90g, mounts are 60% faster than run speed, first mount free, later mounts 20g. Journeyman (epic): level 60, 900g, mounts 200g, +100% speed. Non-racial mounts need only the riding skill; another race's racial mounts need exalted — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever)
- [LIVE] Nov 10: racial vendor mounts are now Bind-on-Pickup. Nov 9: the Felsteed quest grants the riding skill. Nov 25: Paladin and Warlock class mount quests grant Journeyman Riding. Nov 9: the Camp Taurajo to Thunder Bluff flight path no longer clips through objects — [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)
- There are no flying mounts. Riverglades has new flight paths for each faction, and there is a Horde route into Powderfuse Port. Both points come from fan guides, not Blizzard — [WebSearch summary of fan leveling guides](https://wowforeverbuilder.com/guides/leveling/)

**Rested XP and catch-up**
- [PRE] Cozy Sleeping Bag lets players "gain rested experience while not logged in". It requires a level-14 scavenger hunt — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever)
- [LIVE] Nov 17: the Cozy Sleeping Bag "can be placed in specific locations, like hunter traps" and "will now provide a Resting aura" within its area — [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)
- [LOCAL] Attune tracks the Cozy Sleeping Bag chain as a MISC "attunement". Horde chain: 79007 (Barrens) → 79980 (Stonetalon) → turn-in 79974 (Loch Modan). Alliance chain: 79008 (Westfall) → 79980 → 79974 — `Attune\AttuneData.lua`
- Fan guides mention a Camp Tent (rested top-up to 5% of a level, once per hour) and Well-Rested stacks (+1% XP each, 2h, up to 3). Not verified against Blizzard — [WebSearch summary](https://wowforeverguides.com/leveling)

**Group finder**
- [LIVE] Blizzard's hotfix list has only one LFG item, the Nov 20 fix for "some players to see fewer groups than intended when looking for a group". So a native "looking for group" tool exists. The wiki also refers to the in-game Looking for Group tool for dungeon level ranges — [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281); [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever)
- [LOCAL] **ForeverLFG is a third-party addon, not Blizzard's.** Its TOC says Author "Forever", website `https://werko.dev/`, v0.9.36, "Custom - All Rights Reserved", Interface 16001. A companion `ForeverLFG_GroupQuest` addon is also installed — `ForeverLFG\ForeverLFG.toc`

### Inferences
- XP per quest must come from Forever data (QuestieDB `xpDB-traces.lua`), never from Classic tables. The grey-quest floor of 50% makes late or overlevelled turn-ins much more valuable than in Classic. The optimizer should not drop grey quests by default.
- Coordinates from Classic sources are wrong in Mulgore, Eastern Plaguelands, Redridge and Stormwind. Use Forever-native coordinates (QuestieDB Forever build), or apply `LibQuestieDB.EraToForever` explicitly.
- Dungeon quests are now high value. A combined quest plus dungeon route with one pass per dungeon is likely optimal, but kill-XP rules, the 5 entries per hour cap, and the trivial-member zero-XP rule all changed in the first 3 weeks.
- The Forever riding rules (level 40, 60% speed, free first mount) may not differ much from current Classic Era. Verify the Era baseline before modelling this as a Forever-specific change. A route planner should still model a speed step at 40.

### Gaps
- No official Blizzard text was retrieved confirming "no change to XP required per level". The claim rests on the Wowhead retraction headline and fan guides.
- No official source was found for a complete Forever flight path list or for new flight masters per zone.
- The rested XP rate and cap (1.5 levels) were not verified against an official source for Forever.
- The real-world speed of Apprentice riding in Classic Era vs Forever was not compared.
- Which starting zones the Skyborne route continues into after Zephras Isle (level 12 onward) is not documented in the fetched sources.

## 2. New dungeons, raids, attunements and keys vs Classic

### Takeaway
Forever adds 9 new dungeons spread across 13-60 and 2 new raids (Barrow Deeps, 10-man; Hyjal Summit, 20-man) that have no announced attunement. Onyxia keeps the Drakefire Amulet chain, and all three raids unlock on 2026-12-09. Forever also adds new keys tied to City of Dalaran (Dalaran Sewer Key, Tome of Dalaran). Classic keys and attunements (UBRS Seal of Ascension, Onyxia, BRD, Scholomance, Scarlet Key) remain.

### Cited Findings
- [PRE] New dungeons, with Blizzard's official level ranges as quoted by Method.gg:
  - Hall of Thanes 13-18 (under Ironforge)
  - Ruins of Lordaeron 15-20 (above Undercity)
  - Excavation Site: Wetlands 24-29
  - City of Dalaran 28-33 (Alterac Mountains)
  - The Drowned City 35-40 (off Stranglethorn)
  - Krol'dok Stronghold 40-45 (Riverglades)
  - Alcaz Prison 48-53 (Dustwallow)
  - Blackmaw Hold 55-60 (Azshara)
  - Shaper's Terrace 58-60 (Un'Goro)
  - Source: [Method.gg](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-dungeon-xp-explained-and-all-dungeons-by-level). The wiki lists the same set — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever)
- [PRE] Raids (Blizzard, Oct 20 article):
  - Barrow Deeps: 10 players, 8 bosses, beneath Kalimdor with entrances linked to Mount Hyjal, Winterspring and Blackmaw Hold.
  - Hyjal Summit: 20 players, 13 bosses, at Nordrassil.
  - Onyxia's Lair: 40 players. "requires each member of the raid to carry the Drakefire Amulet to enter" and "It does not need to be equipped".
  - All three unlock December 9 for level 60 and are "currently planned to reset each week". No key or attunement is mentioned for Barrow Deeps or Hyjal Summit.
  - Source: [Blizzard Raid Overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24303009)
- Fan sites say the devs hinted at extra steps "to get the full experience" for the new raids, plus a tie-in to an unrevealed Legendary. Not confirmed by Blizzard — [Conquest Capped attunements](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-attunements/)
- [PRE] UBRS needs one raid member with the Seal of Ascension, from a chain started by the Unadorned Seal of Ascension (drops in LBRS). Onyxia needs the Drakefire Amulet for every member, from Warlord's Command (Horde) or Dragonkin Menace (Alliance) — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever)
- [LOCAL] Attune (v16001-46) data contains:
  - 54 dungeon quest groups, including all new Forever dungeons per faction.
  - KEYS: Tome of Dalaran (ID 159; summons optional boss "Lyn the Ignored" in City of Dalaran; starts by killing Archmage Arugal in SFK; "Only one Tome of Dalaran is needed"), Dalaran Sewer Key (154/155; starts with quest 92432 "An Alarming Request", Hillsbrad), Scarlet Key, Blackrock Depths (Shadowforge Key), Scholomance (per faction).

  - MISC: Cozy Sleeping Bag, Aqual Quintessence, Scepter of the Shifting Sands.

### Inferences
- Attune's MC, BWL, Naxx and AQ entries look carried over from Classic. Blizzard announced only Onyxia, Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit for Dec 9, so Classic raids' presence or timing on Forever is unconfirmed. A tracker should flag them "unannounced" rather than active.
- The new raids reach players after the Dec 9 unlock. Any attunement or "extra steps" will likely be discovered then. The key tracker needs a data refresh shortly after 2026-12-09.

### Gaps
- No source on whether MC, BWL, AQ or Naxx exist or are scheduled in Forever (the wiki mentions only "more content in spring and summer 2027").
- The full quest IDs of the Dalaran Sewer Key and Tome of Dalaran chains beyond the first step were not extracted from Attune; they can be read locally.
- No confirmed attunement for Barrow Deeps or Hyjal Summit (as of 2026-11-27).

## 3. Patch cadence since launch and how often quest content changes

### Takeaway
Forever is on client 1.60.1 with frequent silent builds: at least 70888 (Nov 10), 71037 (Nov 20), 71091 (Nov 24) and 71139 (Nov 25). Server hotfixes land almost daily (12 dated hotfix sections between Nov 5 and Nov 25), and nearly every one contains quest fixes. Quest data must be treated as changing daily or weekly, not per patch.

### Cited Findings
- [PRE] Timeline:
  - Beta ended 17:00 UTC Sept 17.
  - Stress test Oct 21.
  - Name reservation Oct 27.
  - Launch at 23:00 UTC Nov 4 (3:00 PM PST).
  - Raids Dec 9.
  - Hardcore in winter 2026/27.
  - More content in spring and summer 2027.
  - Source: [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Forever)
- [PRE] Beta builds include 1.60.1.70058 (Sept 29), 70291 and 70425 (API notes), and 70685 (Oct 28, mage talents) — [MMOEXP](https://www.mmoexp.com/News/wow-forever-beta-patch-notes-guide-1-60-1-70058-tability-audio-cooldown-updates-weather-fixes-september-29.html); [Warcraft Wiki API notes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60.1/API_changes/notes); [wowf.io](https://wowf.io/en/news/patch-notes)
- [LIVE] Live builds: 1.60.1.70888 (Nov 10, inferred from an anchor link), 71037 (Nov 20), 71091 (Nov 24) and 71139 (Nov 25, current). None of them have Blizzard notes; the site read them from client files — [ForeverChanges](https://foreverchanges.pro/patch-notes)
- Other build numbers cited: 70755 (Nov 14, talent data) and 70938 ("live" on Nov 20) per [LFCarry](https://lfcarry.com/guides/wow-forever-patch-notes). These disagree with ForeverChanges about which build was live on Nov 20.
- [LIVE] Blizzard keeps one cumulative hotfix post. It has 12 dated sections: Nov 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 13, 16, 17, 19, 20, 23 and 25. Quest fixes appear on almost every date (e.g. Riverglades, Feralas, Wetlands, Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, Western Plaguelands). Spawn and respawn changes also recur (Nov 9 population reductions; Nov 13 spawn changes in 7 zones) — [Blizzard hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315281)
- [LIVE] Nov 25 also brought an Alterac Valley rework and Balance Druid leveling-talent changes (blue posts) — [wowf.io](https://wowf.io/en/news/patch-notes)
- [LIVE] Questie bundle releases follow the game closely: 12 releases between Nov 7 and Nov 25 (Nov 7, 10, 11, 12, 14, 17, 18, 19, 21, 23, 24, 25), most of them with "[Forever]" items — [Questie releases](https://api.github.com/repos/Questie/Questie/releases?per_page=15)
- [LIVE] QuestieDB commits: 6 on Nov 23, 4 on Nov 24, 10 on Nov 25 and 10 on Nov 26. They include "[db][Forever] Update quests, NPCs, objects and items data" (Nov 23 and 25), "[quest][Forever] Update quest XP data" (Nov 23), "Greatly reduce spawn location dencity for many NPCs" (Nov 25), "fix(forever): regenerate quest relation traces from live recordings" (Nov 24), and many `foreverQuestFixes.lua` edits — [QuestieDB commits (GitHub API)](https://api.github.com/repos/Questie/QuestieDB/commits?per_page=60)

### Inferences
- Quest content (spawns, drop rules, level requirements, rewards, XP) has changed on average every 1-3 days in the first 3 weeks. Recommended refresh: track each QuestieDB release (several per week) and rebuild routes at least weekly. Re-check after every new client build and especially after 2026-12-09.
- Following the user rule, embedded quest XP and level data should carry a build or date stamp (e.g. `xpDB-traces` "Generated at 2026-11-24"). When the running client build (`GetBuildInfo()`) is newer than the stamp, the addon should flag it loudly.

### Gaps
- Blizzard does not publish build numbers in its hotfix posts; build data comes only from fan datamining sites, and they disagree.
- No official statement on a planned patch cadence (e.g. 1.60.2 or 1.61).

## 4. Data sources for Forever quest data

### Takeaway
The most complete, machine-readable and frequently updated Forever quest data is **QuestieDB's Forever flavor**. It is crowd-sourced from QuestieTrace live recordings (2.5M sessions), adds Forever corrections, ships as `QuestieDB_Forever.toc` (Interface 16001, contract version 4), and is released several times a week. No Wowhead Forever quest database was found. Third-party web databases (Metaforge, outof.games, gamer-guides, Mobalytics) exist but disagree on quest counts. wago.tools refused automated requests (HTTP 403), though QuestieDB tooling uses it.

### Cited Findings
- [LOCAL] `Questie_Forever.toc`: `## Interface: 16001`, v12.15.0, `## X-QuestieDB-Contract: 4`. Questie detects Forever with `WOW_PROJECT_ID == WOW_PROJECT_CAMELOT` — `Questie\Questie_Forever.toc`, `Questie\Modules\QuestieCompat.lua`
- [LOCAL] `QuestieDB_Forever.toc`: Interface 16001, v1.1.6, `## X-Contract-Version: 4`, `## X-Flavor: Forever`, about 19 MB. Its header says "GENERATED by `lua generate.lua Forever`", and in "Baked mode" entity reads resolve from a TOC metadata store. Static corrections are folded in at generation, and `foreverQuestFixes.lua` keeps only dynamic (faction, race, class) fixes in the package — `QuestieDB\QuestieDB_Forever.toc`, `QuestieDB\src\corrections\Forever\foreverQuestFixes.lua`
- [LOCAL] Forever support data in QuestieDB:
  - `support\Forever\QuestXP\xpDB-traces.lua` (QuestieTrace, 475,220 trace files, 2,512,170 sessions, generated 2026-11-24) and `xpDB-classic.lua`.
  - `support\Forever\Zones\` (areaIdToUiMapId, uiMapIdToAreaId, instanceIdToAreaId, subZoneToParentZone, dungeons).
  - `support\Forever\DropTables\` (classicItemDrops, itemDropCorrections).
  - `support\Forever\FactionTemplates\`.
  - Corrections in `src\corrections\Forever\forever{Quest,NPC,Object,Item}Fixes.lua`.
  - Source: `D:\Jeux\Battle.net\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\QuestieDB\`
- [LOCAL] QuestieDB 1.1.6 changelog includes: "[Forever] Update quest XP data", "[Forever] Update quests, NPCs, objects and items data", and "[Forever] Add starter for 'Tattered Journal'" — `QuestieDB\CHANGELOG.md`
- [LIVE] QuestieDB repo: commits several times a day (Nov 23-26). Data regeneration uses live recordings ("regenerate quest relation traces from live recordings"). Tooling pulls from wago.tools ("feat(tools): generate pet food support data from wago.tools"). A CI check warns about "unnamed and incomplete Forever entities" — [QuestieDB commits](https://api.github.com/repos/Questie/QuestieDB/commits?per_page=60)
- [LIVE] Questie releases are bundled as `bundle/vQuestie+vQuestieDB` (e.g. `bundle/v12.15.0+v1.1.6`, 2026-11-25) — [Questie releases](https://api.github.com/repos/Questie/Questie/releases?per_page=15)
- Wowhead has a `/forever/` news section, but searches turned up no Forever quest database pages (`wowhead.com/forever/quest=`) — [WebSearch](https://www.wowhead.com/quests/type:128); [Wowhead Forever news](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/wow-forever-official-development-notes-and-class-tuning-383704)

### Inferences
- Best pipeline: read QuestieDB at runtime as a dependency (contract version 4 API, `LibQuestieDB`) instead of copying its data. It is GPL-3.0 (`gpl-3.0.md` in the package), so embedding a copy carries license obligations.
- Data from outside the game (wago DB2, web DBs) should be offered as proposals, not applied silently. This matches the user's rule on external data.
- outof.games says quest level is not in the game data, which means `questLevel` in QuestieDB's trace table is derived. Treat it as approximate for XP math.

### Gaps
- wago.tools coverage of `wowf` and build 1.60.1 DB2 tables was not verified (403).
- No official Blizzard API (Game Data API) for Forever quests was found.

## 5. Addon API notes for Forever (Interface 16001) relevant to a navigation arrow

### Takeaway
Forever runs on a modern (Midnight-derived) client exposed as project "Camelot" (`WOW_PROJECT_CAMELOT`; "Forever" is accepted as a TOC suffix alias). Unlike Classic Era it has `C_SuperTrack` and the native navigation marker, plus `C_Map.SetUserWaypoint`. It also carries Midnight "secret values" and addon restrictions, which force combat and instance guards.

### Inferences
- For MinMaxQuest's arrow, the cheapest path is the native one, as RXP does it: `C_Map.SetUserWaypoint` + `C_SuperTrack.SetSuperTrackedUserWaypoint(true)`, guarded by `C_Map.CanSetUserWaypointOnMap`. A custom TomTom-style arrow is optional (HereBeDragons for distance and bearing).
- Code must guard against secret values and taint: avoid touching protected Blizzard frames in combat, and avoid reading auras in combat or instances.
- Map coordinates for Forever must use Forever UiMapIDs, which may differ from Era for expanded zones. QuestieDB ships `areaIdToUiMapId` and `uiMapIdToAreaId` for Forever.
